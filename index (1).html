The Origin

Born From a Dream

LaToya LaVise didn't plan Women-N-Praise. She dreamed it. She kept waking up with the same vision — the name, the sound, the women. She saw them worshipping from every direction. And the letter N, standing between them all.

"Nobody is creating a space purely for praise and worship. Women carry everything. They deserve a moment to just be in the atmosphere."
— LaToya LaVise, Founder

In 2024, she built it herself. Self-funded. Nearly 100 women showed up — on word of mouth alone. No advertising. No budget. Just the vision. Then came the vocal cord challenge. A season of silence. A comeback that made her stronger.

2026 is the return. Bigger. Official. A real movement.

What Makes This Different

No speaker. No program. No agenda.
Just women, worship, and the presence of God.

Women-N-Praise is a one-of-a-kind experience centered solely on praise and worship. No sermon. No keynote. Just authentic worship, heartfelt prayers, and the kind of healing that flows when women release performance and embrace presence.

Praise & Worship Only

From the first song to the last, this night belongs to worship. No detours. No program. Just the sound that shifts the atmosphere.

Prayer Moments

Interspersed throughout — assigned leaders bringing specific scripture and theme. Space to breathe, receive, and be still.

Spirit-Led Atmosphere

The night moves with the Spirit. Expect the unexpected. Expect breakthrough. Expect to be changed.

Giveaways & Raffles
